♻️ About RecycloLux

RecycloLux helps Luxembourg residents — including newcomers, children, and seniors — learn the country's waste sorting rules through hands-on practice with real items.


Luxembourg's sorting system began in 1995 when Valorlux was founded on 2 October 1995. The rules were significantly expanded in July 2021 (new PMC items) and again with the Waste Act of 2022.


Wrong sorting has real consequences:

  • Red penalty label on your PMC bag — you must re-sort before next collection
  • Administrative fines: €25–€250
  • Unsorted residual bin costs ~€600/year vs ~€220/year for a properly sorted household — a saving of €380/year
